Finite recovery reserve v1
==========================

src/recovery-reserve.js is a fixed, finite inventory, not new puzzle generation.
It exposes NineRealmsRecoveryReserve.candidates(mode,difficulty), get(reserveId),
and workerSource(); returned objects are deep copies. Entries preserve the full
original puzzle, sourceId and generation metadata without inventing Engine IDs.
Provenance identifies the archived source report, SHA256, record index and seed.

216 entries were selected from docs/diversity-audit/v5-audit-30.json and independently
reverified by scripts/build-recovery-reserve.cjs. The original report remains the
source of truth for construction; rebuilding the reserve does not rerun a newer
algorithm and pretend the old seed produced the same puzzle.

Coverage: all 32 fresh-supported modes, easy/medium/hard each at least two.
Classic and king hard have eight each. Disjoint, thermo, XV, greater, lockout and
Xsum hard have four each. Miracle is deliberately excluded: its known one-base
solution space and missing medium/hard proof do not justify invented variety.

Each selected board has a unique solution, correct supported-technique tier for
the seven Hints modes or independently completed RulePath depth tier otherwise.
All applicable RuleQuality thresholds are independently met. Rule depth is not
claimed to be a universal human difficulty rating. No answer-dependent solving
is used for the logical proof. Full original proof and quality evidence is in
  docs/fallback-audit/reserve-construction.json

All 216 bases are pairwise distinct under full classical canonicalization, with
multi boards using conservative sorted component spectra, NOT full multi-grid
graph isomorphism. Across marked modes and jigsaw, 148 geometry layout classes
are distinct even across different difficulty tiers. Capacity evidence is in
  docs/fallback-audit/reserve-capacity.json
Existing recent keys from ordinary play may nevertheless exclude entries. This
reserve is finite and may exhaust. Do not silently relax recent filtering, change
the requested tier, or present a verified reserve board as newly constructed.

Inventory feasibility audit
---------------------------
scripts/audit-fallback-catalog.cjs checked all 1197 nonclassic original bank
entries and the first/last 20 classic entries per labelled tier (120, not all9060).
All1317 checked boards were unique/legal, but bank density labels frequently did
not match real proof tiers. King and disjoint had zero supported-technique hard
boards; thermo/XV/greater/lockout/Xsum zero rule-depth hard; Miracle only easy.
Many old variant pools have just one or three independent classical base classes,
so changing digits alone cannot extend the structural inventory.

scripts/audit-fallback-capacity.cjs checks equivalence capacity using exact pair
comparison after invariant rejection. Evidence: catalog-feasibility.json and
catalog-capacity.json in docs/fallback-audit. Counts from classic are sampled only.

Reproducible checks:
  node tests/recovery-reserve.test.cjs
This independently rechecks all216 entries, compares Node/worker exports, checks
defensive copying and unknown-ID handling. It passed on desktop Linux Node;
this is not a rendering, Windows, mobile or UI-latency certification.

Build script writes reserve data and a non-overwritable evidence file; preserve
existing evidence before intentionally regenerating. Production app integration,
recovery budgets and real recent-history behavior are audited separately.
