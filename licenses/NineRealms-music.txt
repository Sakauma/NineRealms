Homecoming Harmony (归航合鸣)

The compositions, note sequences and synthesis implementation in src/music.js
were created for this project. They contain no third-party recordings, samples,
soundfonts or external music assets. The existing glass event sounds in
src/sound.js are also original procedural synthesis.

The music code and its rendered audio are distributed under the repository's
MIT License. Retain the repository LICENSE when distributing them.

Each composition is a 32-second stereo cycle. The loop buffer includes tails
from the preceding cycle; review recordings contain two cycles with fades only
at the recording's beginning and end. Live playback uses the same renderer.
